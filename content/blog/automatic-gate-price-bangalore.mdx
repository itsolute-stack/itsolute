"What will an automatic gate cost?" is the first thing anyone asks, and the honest answer has two halves: a real starting price for the kit, and the site conditions that decide the rest.

Here are both, for Bengaluru.

## Starting prices

All figures include GST and cover the motor kit:

| Gate type | Motor | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sliding gate | Standard (AC) | ₹39,000 |
| Sliding gate | Premium (DC) | ₹44,500 |
| Swing gate — single arm | DC | ₹45,000 |
| Swing gate — double arm | DC | ₹49,000 |
| Roller gate (wheel-drive) | DC | ₹51,000 |

These are genuine starting points for a standard residential installation, not teaser numbers you will never see again. What they assume is a gate in reasonable condition, power within a sensible run, and no civil work.

## What the kit actually covers

Every figure above buys the same five things:

- **The motor**, sized to your gate's measured weight and width
- **2 remote controls**
- **A safety sensor pair** — photocells across the opening
- **The rack or track** for a sliding gate
- **Welding and mounting**

That last point about safety sensors is worth dwelling on, because it is where cheap quotes get cheap. A quote without photocells is not a discounted version of the same system. It is a different system, one that will close on whatever is in the way. If you are comparing quotes in Bengaluru, ask each one whether a photocell pair is included before you compare the numbers.

## What moves the installation figure

Installation is quoted after the survey because these seven things vary more than the motor does:

**1. Where power is.** A supply at the gate pillar costs nothing to use. A run from the house across a thirty-metre plot is a trench, and on the larger plots north and east of the city that is common.

**2. Whether a plinth is needed.** A sliding gate motor sits on a plinth. If there is nowhere to put one, it has to be cast, and that is civil work.

**3. Track condition.** On a sliding gate, if it drags when you push it by hand, it will drag under power. That track work gets quoted openly rather than absorbed.

**4. Hinge and pillar condition.** On a swing gate, an arm motor transfers real force into the pillar. A pillar sound enough to hang a gate is not automatically sound enough to drive one.

**5. Gate weight.** Not predictable from width. Two gates of identical span can differ substantially once the infill is accounted for, and the motor is sized from the real figure.

**6. Access for the work.** A gate on a narrow lane with no room to park a vehicle takes longer than one on an open frontage.

**7. Whether it has to talk to anything.** Integration with a video door phone, a camera or an existing access system is straightforward but not free.

## Where Bengaluru differs from the rest of the state

Prices here carry a flat ₹5,000 per product over our Kerala figures. That is not a market adjustment — it is the cost of serving Bengaluru from the Bellandur office, and we would rather name it than bury it inside the installation quote where you could not see it.

Everything else is identical: the same motors, the same kit contents, the same two-year-plus manufacturer warranty that varies by brand.

## The add-ons people actually buy

**Battery backup** on a DC motor is two 12V batteries, built in on some brands and a ₹5,500 add-on on others. Worth deciding at installation rather than after the first long cut.

**RFID access** — readers, tags or cards issued per person or per vehicle — is an add-on quoted after the survey. It is what most apartment complexes end up with, because a tag can be deactivated when a flat changes hands and a remote cannot.

**Voice control** through Alexa or Google Home is possible on DC motors on compatible setups. It is not available on AC at all, so if you want it, it decides the tier before anything else does.

## AC or DC, in money terms

The gap between a standard AC sliding motor at ₹39,000 and a premium DC at ₹44,500 is ₹5,500. For that you get smoother starting and stopping, quieter running, battery backup as an option, and the possibility of voice control.

For a gate opened four times a day at a house, AC does the job perfectly well and runs from your inverter connection during a cut. For a gate cycling constantly at a shared entrance, DC ages better and the difference pays for itself. We will tell you which case you are at the survey rather than steering you to the dearer one by default. ## Comparing quotes honestly

Three questions settle most comparisons:

1. **Is it AC or DC?** The two are not interchangeable and the price gap is real.
2. **Does it include a photocell pair?** If not, it is not the same system.
3. **Does it include GST, and is installation inside or outside the figure?**

A quote that is vague on all three is cheaper on paper for reasons you will discover later.
