Real estate runs on two things: leads and follow-up. The offices that grow are the ones where no enquiry gets lost and every branch works off the same information. The offices that stall are the ones where leads live in individual agents' phones and each branch is an island. Here's the complete IT checklist for a real estate firm in Kerala.

## Step 1 — Office hardware

Start with reliable workstations for your agents and back-office staff — desktops or laptops sized for browsers, CRM, document work, and photo-heavy listings running together. Add a good printer for agreements and a solid network. Nothing exotic, but it has to be dependable; agents lose deals when systems are slow. We supply and set up office [hardware](/hardware) with GST invoicing.

## Step 2 — A CRM that fits how you sell

This is the single most important system in a real estate office. A CRM captures every lead, tracks every follow-up, and shows the owner the pipeline at a glance. The mistake most firms make is either not having one, or buying one so complex that agents don't use it.

The right approach: a CRM configured around your actual sales process — how leads come in, how they're qualified, how deals progress — not a generic template. We implement Zoho CRM and others as part of our [software services](/software).

## Step 3 — WhatsApp lead capture and automation

In Kerala real estate, most enquiries come through WhatsApp, phone, and property portals. The problem is they scatter — one agent's phone, another's notebook, a portal inbox nobody checks. Lead capture automation fixes this: enquiries from your website, ads, and WhatsApp flow into one CRM pipeline automatically, get assigned, and trigger follow-up reminders.

This is the difference between chasing leads by memory and never losing one. See our [automation](/automation) page for how we build these flows.

## Step 4 — Connecting multiple branches

If you run more than one office, they need to share information — listings, leads, and pipeline visible across the business, not locked in each branch. We set up secure connectivity and shared systems so the owner sees everything in one place, and one AMC covers every location.

## Step 5 — Backups and document security

Agreements, client details, ID documents, deal records — real estate handles sensitive personal data. Automated encrypted backups protect it against hardware failure, and controlled access keeps it secure. Set both up from the start.

## Step 6 — Ongoing support

Agents work evenings and weekends, when a downed system means a lost deal. An AMC keeps hardware, CRM, network, and automation running with priority response — so IT supports the sales team instead of slowing it down.
