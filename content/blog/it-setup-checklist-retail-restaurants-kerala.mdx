Retail and restaurants live and die by the counter. Billing has to be fast, the network can't drop, cameras have to record, and the owner needs to see every outlet. Get the IT right and the busy hours run smoothly; get it wrong and a frozen counter at peak time costs you customers on the spot. Here's the complete checklist for setting up a shop or restaurant in Kerala.

## Step 1 — POS and billing hardware

The counter is where everything happens, so the hardware has to be reliable and fast:

- A billing PC or POS terminal built for continuous use — not a cheap machine that slows down by evening.
- A receipt/thermal printer, and a barcode scanner for retail.
- A UPS so a power cut doesn't kill a transaction mid-bill.

We supply counter [hardware](/hardware) sized for continuous, fast billing, with GST invoicing.

## Step 2 — Billing and inventory software

Retail and food businesses need different software. A shop needs inventory, barcodes, and stock tracking; a restaurant needs table management, kitchen order tickets, and menu handling. Both need GST-compliant invoicing. We set up billing and inventory software suited to your business as part of our [software services](/software) — configured so the counter is fast and the books are clean.

## Step 3 — A network that doesn't drop at peak hour

Card machines, billing software talking to a server, online orders, staff devices — all depend on a stable network. A ₹1,500 home router in a busy restaurant is a peak-hour failure waiting to happen. A properly sized network keeps the counter moving. See our [networking](/networking) page.

## Step 4 — CCTV

For retail and food businesses, cameras aren't optional — the counter, entrances, stockroom, and kitchen all need coverage, both for security and to settle disputes. The key is CCTV that actually records: installed on its own dedicated network so it doesn't interfere with billing, with reliable storage. See our [CCTV installation](/cctv) page for typical setups and costs.

## Step 5 — Multi-branch reporting

If you run more than one outlet, you need to see them all without calling each shop. Multi-branch reporting pulls sales and stock from every location into a single view, delivered automatically — the difference between running your outlets blind and running them with real numbers. We build this as part of our [automation](/automation) work.

## Step 6 — Backups and ongoing support

Your sales history, inventory, and accounts should be backed up automatically — losing them means rebuilding from paper. And because your busiest hours are exactly when you can't afford downtime, an AMC with priority response keeps the counter running when it matters most.
